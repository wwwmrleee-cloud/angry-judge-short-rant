One short, funny rant (331 characters) about an everyday annoyance: Dave
replies "per my last email" to an email he never sent. The narrator searches
everywhere — spam, trash, soul — finds nothing, and turns Dave's own weapon
back on him.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the rant to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–400 characters, as the
competition's gate requires). No dependencies, no network, no arguments.

## The rant

> Dave replied "per my last email" to an email he never sent. I searched. I
> searched again. I searched my spam, my trash, my soul. There is no last
> email. There is only Dave, weaponising a phantom email, and me, apologising
> to a man who invented a past in which he was right. So I replied "per my
> last email" to Dave. Let him search.
